SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Share Purchase Agreement is entered into on 15 March 2026 between
Alpine Components GmbH ("Seller") and Nordwest Holding AG ("Buyer")
concerning the acquisition of 100% of the shares in Zielgesellschaft
Technik GmbH (the "Target Company").

1. Purchase Price
The total purchase price for the shares shall be EUR 4,200,000, payable
in full within 30 days of signing.

2. Representations of the Seller
The Seller represents and warrants that the Target Company is not
insolvent, is not subject to any bankruptcy or liquidation proceedings,
and has not filed for reorganisation under Austrian insolvency law.

3. Legal Proceedings
The Seller represents that there are no pending or threatened lawsuits,
claims, or arbitration proceedings against the Target Company as of the
date of this Agreement.

4. Intellectual Property
The Seller warrants that the Target Company owns all patents, trademarks,
and know-how used in its business, free and clear of any third-party
claims, and that no such intellectual property is held under licence
from any other party.

5. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all non-public business, technical,
and pricing information disclosed by the other party and shall not
disclose such information to any third party without the prior written
consent of the disclosing party.

6. Liability
The Seller's total liability under this Agreement shall be limited to
EUR 1,000,000, excluding indirect or consequential damages.

7. Term and Termination
This Agreement shall take effect upon signing and may be terminated by
either party upon sixty days' written notice, unless otherwise agreed.

8. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Austria, including
§ 38 UGB and the relevant provisions of the ABGB.
